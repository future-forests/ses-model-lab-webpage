Lang, Kammerer, Puhlmann
PostDoc - Kenton Stutz, technician - Moritz Benka

PhD

A3 Forest Vulnerability & Resilience
A3 — Forest Vulnerability & Resilience
Stand density management as drought adaptation
Schnabel, Bauhus
PhD- David Andermahr
Structural Equation Models · Dendroecology · Remote sensing
  • Quantify how stand density management can reduce drought-related tree mortality in European beech, sessile oak, silver fir, and Douglas fir.
  • Assess drought-related tree stress and mortality across these focal species.
  • Relate stress and mortality to stand density, tree height, age, crown size, and past silvicultural treatments.
  • Identify the optimal stand density “sweet spot” and link it to forest structure and microclimate to guide future drought-adapted forest management.

PhD — Enno Link
Grounded theory · Ethnographic analysis of work · Participant observation · Narrative interviews
  • Follows forest owners, managers and administrators in daily work to understand how tacit, incorporated knowledge is challenged in crisis contexts
