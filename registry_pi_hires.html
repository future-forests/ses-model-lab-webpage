Prof. Dr. Andreas Christen
Chair of Environmental Meteorology · Faculty of Environment and Natural Resources
andreas.christen@uni-freiburg.de
Group Overview
  • WhatDesigns and operates the below-canopy sensor network across ~300 forest plots; develops ML-based microclimate models for the FuFo study region
  • DataWithin-forest air temperature and humidity from sensors; geospatial forest structure predictors (LiDAR, canopy maps); ERA5 reanalysis and GHCN station data as model inputs
  • ModelExplainable ML and LSTM networks for microclimate hindcasting and spatial interpolation; hybrid downscaling for future projections (with Orth, from 2028)

Prof. Dr. Markus Weiler
Chair of Hydrology · Faculty of Environment and Natural Resources
markus.weiler@uni-freiburg.de
Group Overview
  • WhatStudies how water moves through forest soils and catchments under climate change; co-leads the A1 sensor network and leads C3 water redistribution modelling
  • DataSoil moisture, groundwater levels, streamflow, and water quality from 40–50 research catchments; sensor network readings from A1 plots; isotope tracers for water source partitioning
  • ModelProcess-based hydrological model RoGeR for soil water dynamics and lateral redistribution; GIS-based spatial upscaling across the study region; ML for data-driven interpolation alongside Christen

Prof. Dr. René Orth
Chair of Modelling of Biogeochemical Systems · Faculty of Environment and Natural Resources
rene.orth@uni-freiburg.de
Group Overview
  • WhatStudies how forests respond to increasing soil and air dryness; recalibrates process-based ecosystem models for the FuFo region to improve drought response representation
  • DataEddy covariance carbon and water flux measurements; satellite-derived vegetation indices (LAI, NDVI); soil moisture from field sensors and global reanalysis; plant trait maps from Kattenborn
  • ModelQUINCY — process-based ecosystem model recalibrated for stomatal conductance response to dryness; ML approaches (explainable ML) for global upscaling and projections; co-leads A1 ML projection with Christen from 2028
