A1
Adaptive Sensor Networks & Data-driven Modelling
LEAD · Andreas Christen, Fritzi Lang · CONTRIBUTORS · Kammerer, Orth, Puhlmann, Seifert, Stahl, Weiler
  • Builds and operates an adaptive below-canopy sensor network (~300 plots) measuring air temperature, humidity, and water fluxes at high spatial and temporal resolution
  • Develops ML-based approaches for sensor data quality control, station uniqueness ranking, and hindcasting of within-forest microclimates over three decades
  • Produces the observational data foundation — microclimate, soil moisture, water chemistry, water legacy signals — consumed by models in C2 and C3
  • Integration project from 2028: combines ML with QUINCY phenology outputs to project future in-canopy climate under C2 scenarios

C3
Model Framework for Multidimensional Analysis & Stress-Testing of Adaptation Pathways
LEAD · Thomas Seifert, Markus Weiler
  • Develops an integrated interdisciplinary modelling framework — not a monolithic tool, but an assembly of models at variable space and time scales
  • Captures both biophysical processes (forest growth, hydrology, drought) and socio-economic dynamics (markets, agent decisions, material flows) within a coupled SES
  • Facilitates systems understanding: identifies appropriate aggregation levels, model couplings, causal mechanisms, and indicators not directly measurable
  • Enables stress-testing of governance and management approaches under C2 climate and socio-economic scenarios, including climate extremes
  • Builds on CAS properties identified in RA A: uncertainty, heterogeneity, hierarchy, memory, and openness
  • Includes four sequential steps: development, integration, testing, and application of models
