FINN
Käber · Pichler R / torch 		Hybrid forest gap model Inputs
Environment (env_dt)site · per timestepsiteID, year + any number of predictors in natural units (FIA example: mean/max/min temperature °C, annual precipitation mm, precipitation seasonality, precipitation of warmest quarter); must cover every site × year; z-standardised internally (env_autoscale)
Initial cohorts (init_trees)patch · year 0siteID, patchID, year, species, dbh (cm), trees (count), living; assembled into a CohortMat of dimensions sites × patches × cohorts
Calibration data (obs_dt)site × species · per timestepba (m²), dbh (cm), trees (count), growth (relative growth rate), reg (trees ha⁻¹), and mortality as a count pair n_at_risk / n_died; NAs permitted; year 0 excluded (it is the initial state)
Disturbance (dist_dt)site · per timestepoptional; intensity = fraction of patches destroyed that year (0 = none)
Species parameters— · static or learnedper process: regeneration (shade requirement, environmental modifiers), growth (shade, size dependence), mortality (shade, size dependence, growth dependence), competition (height allometry, competition strength); either fixed by the user or estimated from obs_dt
Process specification— · per runone R formula per process linking environmental predictors to that process (e.g. ~ 1 + temp); each process supplied as a mechanistic function, a neural network, or a user-written R function
Simulation configuration— · per runnumber of species, number of patches per site (default 100), patch_size in ha (default 0.1), number of timesteps, cpu or NVIDIA gpu
Timestep length is set by the inventory remeasurement interval and assumed fixed across sites (10 yr in the FIA calibration example). 		Outputs
Mean DBHsite × species · per timestepcm; patch-averaged stand-level state
Basal areasite × species · per timestepm² per patch; divided by patch_size for m²/ha
Tree numberssite × species · per timestepcount per patch; divided by patch_size for N/ha
Available lightsite × species · per timestep%; output of the competition process
Demographic ratessite × species · per timestepgrowth (cm/yr), mortality (%), regeneration count and mean (N/ha)
Per-patch seriespatch × species · per timestepsame variables before patch averaging; gap-model realisations are stochastic
Cohort statecohort · selected timestepsdbh, trees, species per cohort; optional (all, last, or named timesteps) because storing every step is expensive
Calibrated parametersspecies × process · per fitall processes fitted jointly by stochastic gradient descent against a combined likelihood: Gaussian for growth, binomial for mortality, negative binomial for regeneration
Process interpretationprocess · per fitAccumulated Local Effects plots of the learned response of each process to environment and state
